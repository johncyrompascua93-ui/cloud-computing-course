**Analytical Questions**

**Which cloud provider offers the broadest range of services?**
AWS offers the broadest range of services, featuring over 200 fully realized offerings across diverse domains. Its early market entry provided a significant lead in infrastructure breadth and functional depth.

**Which provider best integrates with Microsoft technologies?**
Microsoft Azure integrates best with Microsoft technologies due to native compatibility with Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Microsoft Entra ID. This allows seamless identity management and hybrid licensing for enterprise environments.

**Which provider is strongest in Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes?**
Google Cloud Platform is strongest in Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes. Google originally developed Kubernetes and continues to lead in open-source innovations alongside advanced AI tools like BigQuery and Vertex AI.

**Which cloud platform would you personally choose and why?**
AWS would be my personal choice due to its extensive documentation, mature community support, and industry adoption. Its broad toolset offers maximum flexibility for building scalable full-stack applications.
